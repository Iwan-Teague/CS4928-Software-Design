Does a negatively worded TODO/FIXME comment make developers rate a problem as more urgent, important or effortful? This page shows the survey responses from export_jan_23_no_ip_no_email.csv and the simulated results of the Bayesian model saved in this folder.

1 · Negative vs neutral comments: what participants said

Each participant rated four comments on a 1–5 scale. Two were worded negatively and two neutrally. Which ones were negative depends on the participant's flow, so every comment was seen in both versions by different people.

2 · What the Bayesian model concludes

The authors fitted an ordered-logit model and simulated 100,000 negative-minus-neutral rating differences for each belief group. The bars come from the fitted model (posterior). The outline is what the model assumed before seeing any data (prior). If the bars sit further right than the outline, the data shifted the model towards "negative wording raises the score". The belief-group filter does not apply here, because all three groups are always shown.

3 · Developer beliefs and habits

Questions about negative comments, answered after the vignettes. Statement 3 is the one the analysis uses to put participants into belief groups.

Agree / disagree statements

1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree. The analysis treats 4–5 as Agree, 3 as No opinion and 1–2 as Disagree.

How often…

1 = least often … 5 = most often. The answer labels are not included in the export.
