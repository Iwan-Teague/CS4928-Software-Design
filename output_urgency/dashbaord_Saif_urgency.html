dashbaord_Saif · Urgency

Does a negatively worded TODO/FIXME comment make developers rate the problem as more urgent? This page covers only the urgency rating. It combines the survey responses, a fresh seeded re-run of the authors' Bayesian model (run_urgency.R), and a check of whether the original results reproduce.

The answer, by belief group

From the re-run model: the effect of negative wording on the urgency score (log-odds scale), and how strong the evidence is that it is positive. The groups come from statement 3, "a particularly negative comment means a more important issue".

1 · What participants said

Each participant rated the urgency of four comments on a 1–5 scale. Two of the comments were negative and two neutral, and which ones depended on the participant's flow.

2 · What the model concludes

Ordered-logit model: phi = sentiment[negative?, belief] + constructed[C] + experience·E. The top row shows the model parameter, meaning how far negative wording moves urgency on the log-odds scale. The bottom row turns that into rating differences, from 100,000 simulated pairs of negative vs neutral ratings per group.

Model summary (re-run)

Effect of negative wording on urgency (log-odds). The 89% interval follows the authors' convention. The evidence label comes from brms::hypothesis, using the same thresholds as the original notebook.

3 · Does the original result reproduce?

The authors' simulated urgency differences (in output/, no fixed seed) compared with the seeded re-run (in output_urgency/). Monte-Carlo noise alone should keep the two within about 2 percentage points.
